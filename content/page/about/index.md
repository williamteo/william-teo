Hi, I am William. I work on strategy and skill learning for multi-robot
systems.

Training one robot to do a hard task is difficult enough. In a team, each robot
also has to work out who does what, predict what the others will do, and keep
learning while they are learning too. Sometimes the other agent is not a
teammate. It might be a moving obstacle or an opponent, and the robot has to
work out what it intends and react to it. I work on these problems using
reinforcement learning and game theory.

I am also interested in representation learning, that is, what an agent learns
to pay attention to and what it ignores. This affects what a team can
coordinate on, and whether a skill learned on one task still works on another.

## Learning outside the lab

I skateboard and I learn languages, and I keep running into the same questions
there. Why do some hours of practice count for much more than others? How
should practice be ordered, and what kind of feedback helps? I think these are
the same problems a learning agent faces, just in different words.
